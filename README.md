## AI Content Performance Analysis Across Social Media Platforms
## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes AI-generated content performance across major social media platforms using Python and Power BI. The analysis focuses on platform performance, AI tool usage, AI content types, audience engagement, views, likes, shares, retention rate, and revenue generation. The objective is to identify the most effective platforms, AI tools, and content types for improving engagement and business performance.
## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze AI-generated content performance across different social media platforms.
- Compare views, likes, shares, engagement, and revenue.
- Identify the most frequently used AI tools.
- Evaluate AI content types based on revenue generation.
- Generate business insights and recommendations using Power BI dashboards.

## 📊 Key Insights
- TikTok generated the highest audience reach with approximately 210 million views, while YouTube produced the highest revenue of about 710K, indicating stronger monetization despite fewer views.
- Among all AI tools, CapCut AI was the most frequently used content creation tool. However, Canva AI achieved the highest average - engagement rate, showing that quality of engagement does not always depend on usage frequency.
- Text-to-Video content generated the highest revenue among all AI content types, making it the most profitable content format in the dataset.
- The platform performance summary shows that TikTok recorded the highest average engagement (0.07), followed by Instagram (0.06), while YouTube recorded the lowest average engagement (0.04).
- The production cost versus revenue analysis indicates that YouTube delivered significantly higher revenue for higher production investments, demonstrating better return on investment than the other platforms.
- The overall retention rate of 56.24% indicates that more than half of the audience continued watching AI-generated content, reflecting a satisfactory level of audience retention.
##  💡Recommendations
- Increase investment in YouTube for revenue-focused campaigns because it delivers the highest revenue among all platforms.
- Use TikTok to maximize audience reach and engagement, as it recorded the highest views and average engagement.
- Continue using CapCut AI for efficient content production while combining it with Canva AI to improve audience engagement.
- Prioritize Text-to-Video content because it generated the highest revenue among all AI content types.
- Optimize production budgets by focusing on high-performing platforms and AI tools to maximize return on investment.
##  💡Conclusion
The analysis demonstrates that different platforms serve different business objectives. TikTok is the strongest platform for maximizing audience reach and engagement, whereas YouTube is the best platform for revenue generation. CapCut AI is the most widely used AI tool, while Canva AI delivers the highest engagement performance. Additionally, Text-to-Video content emerged as the most profitable AI content type. These findings provide actionable insights that help businesses optimize AI-generated content strategies, improve engagement, and maximize revenue across social media platforms.
